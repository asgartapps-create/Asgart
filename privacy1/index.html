Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 March 2026

Thank you for choosing Remit (the “Application”). Your privacy is a fundamental priority for us. This policy explains how we collect, use, protect and manage your personal information when you use our mobile application and related services.

1. What data we collect

To provide Remit’s full functionality, including Premium subscription management and access across multiple devices, we collect the following data:

  • Registration data: When you create an account, we ask you to provide a username, an email address and a password, which is encrypted and not stored in plain text.
  • Time-tracking data: The projects, tags and time sessions you record may be securely synchronised with our servers and linked to your account.
  • Technical usage data: We collect basic device information, such as the model and operating system, to diagnose errors and crashes and improve the user experience.

2. Payment processing (Premium version)

If you choose to upgrade to Remit Premium, payment processing is handled entirely by secure, certified third-party services or app stores, such as Google Play Billing, the Apple App Store or Stripe.

Important: We never collect, view or store your full credit card details or financial information. We receive only a “token” or an automatic receipt confirming that the purchase was successful, so we can unlock Premium features, such as advanced historical charts.

3. How we use your information

We use your data exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To create, authenticate and maintain your user account.
  • To provide the app’s features and ensure your data is synchronised across devices.
  • To manage subscriptions and recognise your Premium status.
  • To send service communications, such as password recovery messages or subscription updates.

4. Data security and retention

We protect your data using industry-standard security measures. Connections between the app and our servers are encrypted using secure protocols (HTTPS/TLS). We retain your personal information only while your account is active and for as long as it is necessary to provide the services you request.

5. Account and data deletion

You have full control over your information. You may request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated data, including your projects and time records, at any time.

  • Directly in the app: You can find the “Delete Account” option in your profile settings within the app.
  • By contacting us: Alternatively, you can email us at the address provided at the bottom of this page.

Once deletion has been confirmed, your personal data will be removed from our systems within 30 days, except for any information that must be retained to comply with legal or tax obligations, such as purchase history.

6. Changes to this policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features or legal obligations. We will publish any new version on this page.

7. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about our Privacy Policy or payment methods, or need support with data deletion, please contact us at:

Email: info@asgart.it
